Yang Chen

I am a research scientist at NVIDIA, where I work on post-training for large language models. I received my PhD in computer science from Georgia Tech, where I was fortunate to be advised by Alan Ritter and Wei Xu. Previously, I interned at Google DeepMind (formerly Google Brain) and TTIC.

  • Nemotron 3 Ultra: Open, Efficient Mixture-of-Experts Hybrid Mamba-Transformer Model for Agentic Reasoning
    Core contributor: math proof intelligence and MOPD.

    A 550B total / 55B active parameter MoE hybrid Mamba-Attention model with 1M-token context, post-trained with SFT, RL, and multi-teacher on-policy distillation, reaching up to ~6x higher inference throughput than comparable open LLMs at on-par accuracy.

  • Nemotron-Cascade 2: Post-Training LLMs with Cascade RL and Multi-Domain On-Policy Distillation

    Technical Report, 2026. 600+ likes on Hugging Face.

    We scale Cascade RL and multi-domain on-policy distillation to train Nemotron-Cascade-2-30B-A3B, achieving gold-medal-level results on IMO, IOI, and ICPC World Finals, along with strong performance on ArenaHard and instruction following.

  • Nemotron-Cascade: Scaling Cascaded Reinforcement Learning for General-Purpose Reasoning Models
    Core contributor: math reinforcement learning.
    In Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2026. Oral (top 0.3%). 300+ likes on Hugging Face.
    [models & data] [abstract]

    We scale Cascade RL to train general-purpose reasoning LLMs spanning RLHF, instruction following, math, code, and SWE.
